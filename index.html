Privacy Policy for KanJin

Effective Date: October 2026

KanJin is a Japanese language learning application developed by Popcorn Innovation.

This Privacy Policy explains how KanJin handles information when users access the application, save learning progress, or voluntarily submit a vocabulary issue report.

Information Collection

KanJin does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its learning features.

KanJin does not require:

  • User accounts
  • User registration
  • Profile creation
  • Names or email addresses for normal app use

Local Data Storage

KanJin stores learning-related information locally on the user's device, including:

  • Learning progress
  • Recognized or mastered word identifiers
  • Streak and accuracy information
  • Current word and card position
  • Sound preferences

This locally stored learning information remains on the user's device and is not automatically transmitted to KanJin or Popcorn Innovation.

Users can remove locally stored learning information by using the Reset Progress feature or by clearing the application's storage from the device settings.

Optional Word Reports

KanJin includes an optional feature that allows users to report possible issues with vocabulary content, meanings, kanji, readings, romaji, word matching, or JLPT level information.

When a user chooses to continue with a report, KanJin opens a Google Form containing information relevant to the reported vocabulary item.

The report may include:

  • The selected issue category
  • Optional details entered by the user
  • The KanJin word identifier
  • The English meaning
  • The displayed kanji
  • The Japanese reading
  • The romaji reading, when available
  • The associated JLPT level
  • The correct or incorrect reveal result
  • The KanJin app version

Users can review and edit the information in the Google Form before submitting the report. Opening the form does not automatically submit a report.

KanJin does not require a name or email address when submitting a vocabulary issue report. Users should not include personal, confidential, or sensitive information in the optional details field.

Submitted reports are used only to review, correct, maintain, and improve KanJin's learning content and application experience.

Google Forms is an external service operated by Google. Information submitted through the report form is processed using Google's services and is subject to Google's applicable privacy practices.

Data Sharing

KanJin does not sell user information.

Local learning progress is not shared with third parties. Information is sent externally only when a user voluntarily chooses to submit a vocabulary issue report through Google Forms.

Advertising

KanJin does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services to track users.

Analytics

KanJin does not currently use third-party analytics services to collect personal information or track learning activity.

Internet Access

KanJin's primary learning features are designed to work without an internet connection.

An internet connection is required when a user chooses to open or submit the optional Google Forms vocabulary report.

Children's Privacy

KanJin does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Users should not enter personal or sensitive information into the optional vocabulary-report details field.

Data Security

Local learning information is stored on the user's device. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device.

Vocabulary reports are transmitted to Google Forms using an HTTPS connection. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Local learning information remains on the device until it is reset by the user, cleared through the device settings, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

Submitted vocabulary reports may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the reported issue, improve KanJin, maintain correction records, and prevent duplicate work.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KanJin's functionality or information-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
