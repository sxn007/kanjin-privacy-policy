Privacy Policy for KanJin

Effective Date: August 2026

KanJin is a Japanese language learning application developed by Popcorn Innovation.

Information Collection

KanJin does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

KanJin does not require:

  • User accounts
  • User registration
  • Profile creation

Local Storage

KanJin stores learning-related information locally on the user's device, including:

  • Learning progress
  • Mastered words
  • Streak information
  • Sound preferences

This information remains on the device and is not transmitted to external servers.

Data Sharing

KanJin does not sell, transfer, or share user information with third parties.

Advertising

KanJin does not display advertisements.

Analytics

KanJin does not use analytics services to collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

KanJin does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

All learning information is stored locally on the user's device.

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.
